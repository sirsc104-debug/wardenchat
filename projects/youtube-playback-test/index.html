Videos to test

Paste YouTube links or 11-character IDs, one per line. Anything after # is your label (for example # works on youtube.com or # blocked at school), and is carried into the results so you can compare expected and actual behaviour. Watch, youtu.be, /shorts/, /embed/ and /live/ links all work. Shorts links give a true vertical test; regular videos are shown letterboxed in the 9:16 player.

About scrolling over the video: the player is a YouTube iframe, and the browser sends scroll, clicks and keys that happen over it to YouTube, not to this page. By default, scroll anywhere outside the picture (on the controls, beside it, or on this panel’s margin) and use the keyboard while focus is on this page. The gesture layer option lets scroll, swipes and clicks over the picture reach this page instead, so YouTube’s on-video controls stop responding. It exists only to measure that experience; check YouTube’s embedded-player policies before using that pattern in SirSC Scroll.
